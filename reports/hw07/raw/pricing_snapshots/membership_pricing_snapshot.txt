Capital Bikeshare: Member-only perks
For less than a dollar a day, you get unlimited 45-minute rides on classic bikes, reduced ebike prices, free unlocks, and a whole lot more.
Bike Angels
Angels take bikes from crowded stations to empty stations, making the system work better for everyone. In return, they earn points toward ebike credits, free membership extensions, and more. Check it out.
Early access
Get access to new features before anyone else, and get an opportunity to tell us how we can improve the rider experience.
Free parking near full stations
If a station has three or fewer docks available, members can park their ebike within 40 meters of the station for free.
More, more, more
Reserve-ahead features. Five free unlocks for friends. Being a member means you get more good stuff.

Get started with the app
Single rides
Great for one-way trips. Just download the Capital Bikeshare or Lyft app and you’re ready to roll. Just $1 to unlock plus $0.15/minute for classic bikes or $0.35/minute for ebikes.
$1 + $0.15/ mins
Day pass
When you’ve got lots to do. $10/day includes unlimited 45-min classic bike rides within a 24-hour period. Ride longer than 45 minutes and pay $0.05/minute. No unlock fees and a discounted rate of $0.15/min on ebikes.
