Example Bike Share Pricing
Single rides
Just $1 to unlock plus $0.15/minute for classic bikes or $0.35/minute for ebikes.
Monthly membership
Monthly membership: $10 per month.
Members ride classic bikes free for the first 45 minutes of every ride and pay no unlock fee.
Members who ride longer than 45 minutes pay $0.05/minute after the first 45 minutes.
Members pay a discounted rate of $0.15/min on ebikes.
Day pass
$10/day includes unlimited 45-minute classic bike rides within a 24-hour period.
